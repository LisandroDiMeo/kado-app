Kado

Open-source spaced repetition flashcards

Learn and retain knowledge with your choice of SM2 or FSRS scheduling algorithms. Import Anki decks, study rich text cards, track your progress, and even sync to an ESP32 hardware device.

Features

Spaced Repetition

SM2 and FSRS scheduling algorithms with per-deck selection. Four-button rating with interval preview.

Anki Import

Import .apkg decks with full template rendering, media extraction, and support for both legacy and modern formats.

Rich Content

HTML rendering with bold, italic, colors, headings, lists. Image support and adjustable font scaling.

Deck Management

Partition large decks, clone, create reversed decks, and bulk edit with saved find/replace rules.

Statistics

Track review progress, card states, and retention. Visualize your learning with detailed analytics.

ESP32 Hardware

Sync flashcard decks to an ESP32 device over WiFi for offline review on dedicated hardware.
