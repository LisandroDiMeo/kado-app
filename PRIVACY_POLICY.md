# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** March 16, 2026

## Overview

Kado is a spaced repetition flashcard app. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how the app handles your data.

## Data Collection

Kado does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All your data — including decks, cards, review history, and settings — is stored locally on your device.

## Network Usage

The app connects to the network only in the following scenario:

- **ESP32 Device Sync:** When you explicitly choose to sync decks to a companion ESP32 hardware device, the app communicates with the device over a local WiFi connection. This communication happens directly between your phone and the device on a local network. No data is sent to any external server.

## Third-Party Services

Kado does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, advertising, or crash reporting services.

## Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device using an on-device database. If you uninstall the app, all data is deleted.

## Children's Privacy

Kado does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the changes will be reflected in this document with an updated date.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can open an issue on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/LisandroDiMeo/kado).
